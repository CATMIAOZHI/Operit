# Verify requirements and connections

Check consequential entry, transit, airport and transfer claims against the exact traveler and journey. A neighboring leg's rules may not apply.

## Identify each leg

- Record airport codes, cities, countries or territories, border or customs areas, local dates and times, operating carrier and flight number. Include known terminals and the next connection or entry step.
- Resolve codes and classifications from current authoritative sources. Check domestic, international, airside and preclearance labels separately. Cross-country travel is normally international; exceptions and special customs areas need official evidence.
- Keep URLs and check dates and times for terminals and procedures. Opening an old page today does not make its rules current.

## Ask only necessary eligibility details

- Citizenship, passport issuer and type, residency or visa status, purpose, and stay or transit duration may matter. Never infer documents from the departure country.
- If essential facts are missing, explain the general process and leave eligibility unresolved. Keep passport and identity numbers out of planning notes.

## Match sources to claims

- Destination and transit government, immigration or border authorities establish visa, entry, transit, passport, arrival-form and customs rules. Home-government travel advice adds context.
- Operating airlines or airports establish terminals, baggage, check-in and connections. Service operators establish fast-track or transfer eligibility, hours, meeting points, inclusions and prices. Current maps or operator data support ground estimates.
- Check applicability to the travel date. Keep URLs, publication or update dates when available, and check dates. Reviews cannot establish official rules.
- State unverified claims and make dependent recommendations conditional. Historical queues are not live waits; assess needs and terms before recommending fast-track.

## Carry checks forward

Transit permission does not establish a feasible connection. Include immigration, bags, terminal changes, recheck and last ground services; future traffic remains estimated.

When booking is requested, read `booking-handoff.md` here. Carry legs, eligibility and sources; match transfers to the exact airport, terminal, arrival, party, luggage and destination. Put unsupported steps in the trip checklist. Recheck volatile rules before booking and departure.
