# Maintain a workable itinerary

Read this for multi-day plans, revisions, important logistics, open work and itinerary files. Inspiration can stay light; check critical conditions before calling a plan feasible.

## Keep one current plan

- Record exact dates, local timezones, overnight bases, party, budget, pace, interests, exclusions and fixed commitments. Include ages, mobility or meal needs only when relevant.
- Track three dimensions separately: decision—proposed, selected or rejected; evidence—unchecked, estimated or verified, with source and check time; seller status—unchecked, available, awaiting confirmation, confirmed, failed, cancelled or unknown.
- Selection is not confirmation. Do not call unchecked items secured, available or booked. Requests, waitlists and temporary holds are not confirmations.
- Proposals become selected only through user choice or delegated authority. Silence, questions and a request to build a schedule do not select every item. Keep rejected options in history, outside the active schedule.
- Treat bookings as fixed. Preserve user-reported bookings labeled “not independently verified”; never silently rebook them.

## Track remaining work

- Keep a compact checklist across turns: outcome, owner, status, next step, evidence and material deadline. Use “unknown” for unverified deadlines and “none” only when none applies.
- Planning owns research and feasibility. Booking submission belongs to its skill; read `booking-handoff.md` here. Give the user only necessary decisions, private input, approvals or unsupported actions.
- Use understandable statuses such as researching, needs decision, ready to book, booking, user action, blocked, confirmed and completed. Drop work only when the user removes it.
- After the first plan, material changes or a request for remaining work, show Component, Current plan, Status and Next step. Avoid repeating it after minor exchanges; use a short spoken summary for voice.
- Use `orbit_tracking` for concrete commitments and complete them only when resolved. Tracking does not monitor. For requested reminders or checks, read `docs/scheduling-and-watching.md`, create an `orbit_task`, avoid duplicates and verify timezone and next run. Do not promise delivery while the phone is offline or suspended.
- Distinguish a delivered planning brief from a fully booked trip. Do not call all work complete while required items remain open.

## Revise without losing decisions

- Read the current record first. Change relevant items, preserving unrelated days, limits, rejected ideas and confirmations. Never add a stop merely to fill space.
- Moving an item preserves its identity and choice. Recheck hours, transport, tickets and dependent steps. Replacements start as proposals; retain the old decision.
- Name gaps left by removal. Undo restores planning content, not purchases, cancellations or messages. Briefly summarize changes and checks still needed.
- Keep concurrent trips in separate files. Reread shared memory before a relevant change; temporary travel details are not lasting preferences.

## Make each day feasible

- Resolve the year, local timezone and overnight base or arrival and departure points. Missing essentials require a conditional sketch, not a claim of feasibility.
- Cluster nearby stops. Give a time or honest window, place, duration, transport from the previous stop, buffers, meals or rest, known costs and booking status, with a brief reason for fit.
- Verify opening days, last admission, reservation windows, check-in and connections. Show actual arrival dates. Future journey times are estimates unless they identify a published scheduled service.
- Carry age rules, walking load, stroller or mobility access, breaks and meals through every day. Fix overload by reducing or rearranging stops, not shortening realistic durations.
- Propose a backup for weather, closure, scarcity or tight connections. Map routes do not prove seats are available.

## Verify consequential facts

- Confirmations establish their bookings; sellers establish their own current inventory and terms. Officials establish rules and hours; reviews help assess fit.
- Resolve the exact place or service. Keep URLs, check dates and times, and material assumptions. Open important links to confirm their destination.
- Dead links, login failures or wrong pages do not establish closure or sellout. Find current sources or mark uncertainty. A working homepage does not prove availability.
- Published admission or pass prices support estimates. Recheck date inventory, fees and terms at booking. Prefer the authority responsible when sources disagree; disclose unresolved differences.
- Never invent venues, services, prices or links. Label unpublished seasonal schedules. Recheck volatile facts before booking and departure; read `travel-fact-verification.md` for borders and airports.

## Calculate useful costs

Verify a pass's exact product, duration, validity, class or zones, eligibility and official price. Compare only planned uses, checking inclusion, discounts, reservations, supplements and exclusions.

Show adult and child quantities, taxes, uncovered costs and arithmetic. Cite exchange-rate sources and dates. Recalculate labeled subtotals. Missing material prices or eligibility prevent a savings claim; estimates are not live offers.

## Save and deliver

Maintain one trip file under `files/` with `orbit_file`. When the user requests or accepts file delivery, prepare it for use. Read before rewriting, preserve unrelated content and do not assume automatic file history.

Include local times, transport, buffers, costs, booking states, sources and unresolved choices. Distinguish confirmations, user reports, proposals and estimates. Keep brief change notes; avoid competing current copies.

Use verified photos or schematic maps under `planning-kickoff.md`; retain a usable list. Deliver with `orbit_file action=send`. Export to HTML, DOCX or PDF when needed and inspect the result; success does not guarantee layout.

Keep files private. Share or publish only as explicitly requested under current privacy and sending rules. Never invent public links or call them private. Exclude secrets, payment details and unnecessary identity numbers.
