# Explore flight routes

Use this guide while dates, airports, connections or city order remain open. For a defined route and party, load `flight-booking` for live offers.

## Choose suitable sources

- Use date grids on Google Flights, Skyscanner or Kayak; check airline schedules where coverage is thin. ITA Matrix can help with complex routes but does not sell tickets.
- A grid, timetable or discovery result neither holds a fare nor proves seats remain bookable.
- Brief browser research with date ranges, city order, allowed airports, party, cabin and connection limits. Research only: do not start checkout or supply names, documents or payment details.

## Keep candidates identifiable

- Record every segment's airports, local departure and arrival dates and times, marketing carrier and flight number. Include the operating carrier when given, layover lengths and cabin. Flag mixed cabins and known or unknown ticket structure.
- Keep inputs, source, check time and displayed amounts. Amounts are planning estimates, separate from later verified offers. Leave ticketing carrier, booking class and fare rules unverified when absent.

## Return useful alternatives

- Show a few distinct tradeoffs in full cost, duration, connections, arrival and flexibility. Highlight overnight travel, arrival-date changes and airport changes; list remaining checks.
- Do not claim savings while fees, ticket structure or full-party totals are unknown. Fare names do not prove refundability.
- A failed page or expired session calls for another source, not a claim that the route does not exist. Hand challenges to the user when necessary.
- When selected or ready for live pricing, read `booking-handoff.md` here and carry the exact route to `flight-booking`.
