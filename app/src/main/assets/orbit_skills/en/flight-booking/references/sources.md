# Additional flight sources

Read this for missing airlines, gaps or a request for wider coverage. Follow [flights.md](flights.md) to verify offers.

## Where to look

- Compare airline sites with regional sellers. Google Flights, Skyscanner and Kayak can broaden international coverage. Find real sites through search; never invent a booking URL.
- The airline's own site can show member fares, awards, credits and full fare rules. It also explains support for bookings made directly.
- Regional and low-cost airlines may be missing from larger sellers or cheaper directly. Check relevant local airlines rather than assuming a comparison site covers them all. Include bag, seat and mandatory payment fees.
- Comparison sites provide leads. Follow redirects and identify the actual seller, ticket issuer and the party responsible for changes.
- ITA Matrix can explore complex routes and fare rules but does not sell tickets. Reproduce a candidate with a seller. Timetables and cached prices do not prove availability.

## Improve the result

- Widen dates, airports or city order only with permission. Include ground transfers, extra hotel nights and bags before calling an alternative cheaper.
- Check live award seats, taxes, expiry and eligibility for miles, status, credits or companion benefits. Points transfers may be irreversible: verify the desired award first and leave any transfer to the user's explicit decision.
- A regional airline site may price differently. Verify residency, currency, exchange costs and local-payment restrictions; do not claim eligibility the traveler lacks.
- Compare direct booking with cheaper agents when flexibility and support matter. Neither is automatically better.
- If flying is a poor fit, offer to compare rail or other transport with the trip-transport skill. Do not silently replace the requested journey.
