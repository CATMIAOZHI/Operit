# Trip transport

## Choose how to get there

- Settle the route, local dates and times, travelers (children's ages), luggage, any car or bike, and which times are fixed. Check the trip plan and existing confirmations first.
- Compare door-to-door time and total cost: transfers, parking, tolls, fuel or charging, and an extra hotel night a slow option can add. Plan in the destination's local time.
- Name what would make an option fail, such as a last ferry before the flight lands or a pickup desk that closes early.
- Only an operator or seller showing a live departure establishes availability. Timetables, route planners and old bookings do not.

## Trains and buses

- Check the national operator for live times and fares, and whether seats need a reservation. Compare a rail pass with point-to-point tickets for the actual journeys.
- Long-distance buses can beat rail on price or reach; check the luggage rules.

## Rental cars

- Confirm the driver's age, license country and years held. Many countries need an International Driving Permit.
- Compare the full price: one-way and after-hours fees, young-driver fees, airport surcharges, mileage limits, fuel policy and the deposit.
- Explain cover plainly, and check a credit card's rental cover before the user declines the counter's cover.
- Flag local rules that cost money: restricted zones, vignettes, congestion charges and electronic tolls billed with a fee.

## Ferries, bikes and rides

- Ferries separate foot passengers from vehicles, and vehicle space sells out first. Weather cancels sailings, so allow a buffer before a flight.
- For short city trips, compare bike share, walking and transit.
- After a late arrival a prebooked transfer can beat surge pricing; confirm the meeting point and how long the driver waits.

## Book

- Book where the live option was shown. Orbit cannot pay: stop at the payment page and hand the browser to the user. When a result is uncertain, check the bookings before trying again.
- Report the confirmation number, departure point, local time, what to bring (ID, license, ticket) and the cancellation deadline.
