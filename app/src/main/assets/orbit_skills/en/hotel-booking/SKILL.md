# Hotel booking

## Find a stay that fits

- Settle destination, dates, rooms and who sleeps in each (children's exact ages), budget, location and accessibility needs. Guest identity and payment wait until the user picks a rate.

## Compare full stays

- Compare the property's own site with major sellers such as Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda and Google Hotels. Add a rental platform for a kitchen, a group or a long stay. Local sites and regional chains often have rooms the global sites lack.
- Rank by the full-stay total with mandatory taxes and fees, together with location and terms. Refundable, prepaid and pay-at-property rates are different offers, even at one hotel.
- Verify any amenity that decides the choice; a property description does not prove a given room has it. Star class and guest ratings are different things.

## Book

- Book with the seller of the verified rate and have the user check guest details.
- Orbit cannot pay for the user. Stop at the payment page and hand the browser to the user. If the price rises or terms get worse, tell the user first.
- When a result is uncertain, check the bookings before trying again.
- Report the confirmation number, address, check-in and check-out times, the free-cancellation deadline and what to bring; add reminders with orbit_task when useful.
