# Compare and book stays

Read this for accommodation. Add flights, activities or wider planning only when requested.

## Dates and occupancy

- Establish destination or property, local check-in and check-out dates, rooms and guests per room. A named night can mean one night; state that assumption.
- Obtain each child's exact age. Never guess or search children as adults. Search again when occupancy changes, including child eligibility, extra beds and meal charges.
- Apply stated location, bed, budget and payment preferences. One stay is not a permanent preference or evidence of accessibility needs or acceptance of nonrefundable rates.
- For broad destinations, start around the user's purpose, venue or known location and say where. Refine after the first shortlist rather than withholding results for a questionnaire.

## Match offers

- Use web search and the browser to compare property sites, major sellers and regional platforms. International examples include Booking.com, Agoda and Expedia. Orbit has no dedicated hotel connector.
- Consider rentals for kitchens, groups or long stays. Include cleaning, service fees, deposits, house rules and check-in arrangements; explain differences from hotel service.
- Match dates, occupancy, room, bed, meals, cancellation and payment timing. Cheaper agents may omit breakfast, points or flexibility; direct booking is not automatically better.
- Verify with the actual seller. Discount labels, snippets and nightly teaser rates do not establish the total. Recheck any changed room or dates after a redirect.

## Rank the full stay

- Compare the entire stay with mandatory taxes and fees. Separate due-now, due-at-property, deposits and card holds. Keep estimated totals or unknown mandatory fees and cancellation rules out of the verified shortlist.
- Free cancellation must still be available. State the deadline in the property's local date, time and timezone. Refundable, nonrefundable, prepaid and pay-at-property offers are distinct.
- Verify the exact room, bed, meals, parking, view, check-in times and decisive amenities. A property description does not prove that room has a kitchen, accessible bathroom or cot.
- Verify the address and travel time to what matters. State walking, transit or driving assumptions; straight-line distance is not a commute.
- Separate star class from guest ratings. Read recurring recent complaints and never fill gaps with brand reputation.

## Present and reserve

- Give two or three stable labels. Use a short comparison table across sellers: room, full total, payment timing, cancellation deadline, amenities, location and seller link. Explain the tradeoff. Show a map when requested using verified coordinates.
- Continue with the chosen seller and refresh the offer. Explain price increases or worse terms and obtain agreement; do not switch room or property.
- Review property and address, dates, occupancy, exact room and rate, total, payment timing, holds, inclusions and cancellation timezone. Have the user check guest details.
- If ordering authority is unclear, use `orbit_request_approval` and wait. Do not repeat unchanged authorization. Share only required personal details.
- Use `orbit_vault` for secure login. Hand passwords, verification and payment to the user with `orbit_browser_handoff`, then end the turn. Orbit cannot handle cards, including guarantees for pay-at-property rates.
- Inspect the page and account after handback. Reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying. Report pending states accurately.
- Confirm the reference, room, address, local check-in times, amount due at property and cancellation deadline. Save requested reminders with `orbit_task` before claiming they are scheduled.
